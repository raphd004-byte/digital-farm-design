Our Story

Grown with care on the slopes of Kahawa.

Mama Njeri's Organic Farm sits on the fertile slopes of Kahawa, where the red volcanic soil and morning mist give our fruits their rich flavour.

We started in 2018 with just two trees and a small patch of beans — today we grow tree tomatoes, avocados, mangoes, passion fruit, French beans, and macadamia nuts using only organic methods. Every harvest is hand-picked at peak ripeness and packed the same day so you get fruit that tastes like the farm it came from.

We believe in honest farming: no shortcuts, no chemicals, just good soil and careful hands. When you buy from us, you're not just getting fresh fruit — you're supporting a small family farm and the people who tend it.
